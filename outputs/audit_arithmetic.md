# Audit arithmetic (Section 5.2 of the paper)

This appendix gives the arithmetic behind the estimated recoverable coverage of Section 5.2.

The two groups were sampled in near-proportion to their size (39 of 296, 13.2%, and 11 of 87, 12.6%). The sample proportions of construction reports are 23/39 = 0.5897 and 3/11 = 0.2727, and the group weights are 296/383 = 0.7728 and 87/383 = 0.2272. The stratified proportion is therefore 0.7728 × 0.5897 + 0.2272 × 0.2727 = 0.5177. Its variance is the sum over the two groups of W² (1 − f) p (1 − p) / (n − 1), where f is the sampling fraction. The sum is 0.0042, the standard error is 0.0648, and the 95% interval is 0.5177 ± 1.96 × 0.0648, or 0.391 to 0.645 (normal approximation, with only 11 records in the production group). The sample comes from the 383 records with a genuine narrative among the 456 unclassified records, and the proportion is scaled by 383/1,759 = 0.2177. The estimated residual is 0.5177 × 0.2177 = 0.1127, or 11.3 percentage points (approximately 198 DDRs). Adding the observed coverage (1,303/1,759 = 0.7408) gives an estimated recoverable coverage of 85.3%. Scaling the interval in the same way gives 0.7408 + 0.391 × 0.2177 = 0.826 and 0.7408 + 0.645 × 0.2177 = 0.881, a 95% interval of 82.6 to 88.1%.

For comparison, the unstratified sample proportion is 26/50 = 0.520 with a Wilson-score 95% interval (Wilson 1927) of 0.385 to 0.652. Scaling by 0.2177 gives a residual of 0.1132 (approximately 199 DDRs) and an estimated recoverable coverage of 85.4%, with the interval 0.7408 + 0.385 × 0.2177 = 0.825 and 0.7408 + 0.652 × 0.2177 = 0.883, or 82.5 to 88.3%. The two estimates differ by 0.1 percentage point.
