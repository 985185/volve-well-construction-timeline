@@ Appendix B: Audit Arithmetic | The sample proportion is 26/50 = 0.520
The sample proportion is 26/50 = 0.520, with a Wilson-score 95% interval of 0.385 to 0.652. The sample comes from the 383 records with a genuine narrative among the 456 unclassified records, and the proportion is therefore scaled by 383/1,759 = 0.2177. The estimated residual miss is 0.520 × 0.2177 = 0.1132, or 11.3 percentage points (approximately 199 DDRs). Adding the observed rate (1,303/1,759 = 0.7408) gives an estimated attainable classification rate of 85.4%. Propagating the Wilson interval gives 0.7408 + 0.385 × 0.2177 = 0.825 and 0.7408 + 0.652 × 0.2177 = 0.883, a 95% interval of 82.5 to 88.3%. Weighting the two strata by their population shares (296/383 and 87/383) gives a sample proportion of 0.518 and an attainable rate of 85.3%. The result does not depend on whether the estimate is stratum-weighted.
